About

Three things decide whether a cloud estate is healthy: uptime, security and cost. I work on all three.

I'm Vivekkumar Natarajan, an Azure cloud engineer in Bengaluru. Since 2023 I've supported production cloud estates for enterprise customers at Xencia Technology Solutions, where the day runs on deployments, incidents and the quiet work of making sure nothing breaks at 3 a.m.

That means sizing and resizing virtual machines, wiring site-to-site and point-to-site VPNs, setting budgets and alerts before a bill surprises anyone, running patch cycles, and tracing incidents back to a root cause that stays fixed.

I'm certified as a Microsoft Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104) and work alongside operations and development teams on migrations and environments that need a careful pair of hands.

Outside the ticket queue I'm usually reading up on Azure networking and governance — the parts of the platform that quietly decide how much everything else costs.
